Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the DevOps on Fire game for Android (package com.devopsonfire.game), published on Google Play by DevOps on Fire (“we”, “us”). Short version: the game does not collect, store, or share any personal or device data.

At a glance

Personal data collectedNone
Data shared with third partiesNone
Internet accessNot used. The game works fully offline.
Device permissionsNone requested
Accounts or sign-inNone
Ads, analytics, trackingNone
In-app purchasesNone

Information we collect

We do not collect any information. The game runs entirely on your device. It does not ask for your name, email, or any other personal details, and it does not create device identifiers, advertising IDs, or usage logs.

Device permissions

The game does not request any Android permissions. It has no access to your contacts, camera, microphone, location, photos, or files, and it does not request internet access. The game plays sound effects but never records audio.

Analytics, advertising, and third-party services

The game contains no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, or tracking SDKs, and no third-party services that collect data.

Game progress

Lesson progress is kept in memory only while the game is open and is cleared when you close it. Nothing is written to permanent storage or sent off your device.

Data retention and deletion

Because we do not collect or store any data, there is nothing for us to keep or delete. Uninstalling the game removes it completely from your device.

Google Play

Google Play handles downloads and updates under the Google Privacy Policy. As the developer, we may see aggregated, anonymous statistics that Google provides in the Play Console, such as install counts and crash rates. These statistics do not identify you, and we do not combine them with any other information.

Children

The game is suitable for a general audience. It does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country).

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR or CCPA), you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your personal data. Since we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing to access or delete, but you can contact us with any questions.

Security

The game is distributed through Google Play and signed by us. With no data collection or network connections, no personal information about you is at risk through the game.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the game changes how data is handled, we will update this page and its effective date before that version is released.
